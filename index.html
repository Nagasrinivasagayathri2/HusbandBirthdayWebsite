A LITTLE NOTE FROM MY HEART

To my husband...

If someday we look back at this little website, I hope we smile at how young we were, how much we were still learning about each other, and how beautifully our life slowly unfolded.

I don't know what the coming years will look like. I don't know how many new places we will see, how many silly arguments we will have, how many ordinary mornings will become memories without us even realizing it.

But there is one thing I know. I want to experience all of it with you.

I want the quiet days. The busy days. The days when everything goes exactly as planned, and the days when nothing does. I want the random laughs, the late-night conversations, the little celebrations, the countless ordinary moments that nobody else will ever know about.

And when life changes us, I hope we never forget the two people who started all of this with nothing but a beginning and a little faith.

Today is your birthday, but somewhere in my heart, I feel like I am celebrating something bigger too — the life that is waiting for us.

There are still so many places we haven't seen, so many dreams we haven't spoken about, so many photographs we haven't taken, and so many chapters we haven't written.

So let's take our time. Let's make mistakes, learn, laugh, grow, and keep choosing each other through every version of life.

I don't want a perfect life.
I just want a life that feels like ours.

Happy Birthday, my love.
Here's to everything we haven't lived yet. ❤️

Forever yours,
Gayathri ♡
